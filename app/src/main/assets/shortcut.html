Add “Play on TV” to your Share menu

After this one-time setup, you can tap Share → Play on TV on any page in Safari and it starts on the TV. No QR code needed.

Your TV's address http://{{ADDR}}/play
  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to make a new shortcut. Tap its name at the top and rename it Play on TV.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (Details) button and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Tap Done.
  3. At the top it now says “Receive Any input from Share Sheet”. Tap Any and keep only URLs and Safari web pages.
  4. Tap Search Actions, find Get URLs from Input, and add it.
  5. Search again for Get Contents of URL and add it below. Tap its blue URL field and paste the address above: http://{{ADDR}}/play
  6. Tap the › arrow on that action to show more options. Set Method to POST and Request Body to JSON.
  7. Tap Add new field → Text. For the key type url. For the value, tap it and pick the URLs variable (from the step before).
  8. Tap Done. Now in Safari, open a stream page, tap Share, and choose Play on TV.
The first time, iPhone may ask to allow Play on TV to connect to the TV or to find devices on your local network. Tap Allow. StreamLink must be open on the TV.

Keep the TV's address the same

The shortcut remembers the address above. Your router can hand the TV a different one after a restart, which would break the shortcut. To prevent that, open your router's settings (usually its app, or a web page like 192.168.1.1) and look for DHCP reservation, Address reservation or Static IP. Reserve the TV's current address for it.

If the shortcut ever stops working, check that the address under the QR code on the TV still matches the one in your shortcut, and update it if not.
